# GWT, AI authoring, and human understanding

As AI becomes better at producing code, the important collaboration artifact is
behavior that people can read, run, and review. GWT makes that artifact executable:
typed domain rules, concrete scenarios, and source-backed decision evidence live
in one language. A separate generated implementation is not the source of truth.

AI can inspect contracts, propose changes, run validation, and prepare comparisons.
People still determine intended policy and whether the evidence supports it.
Readable syntax is a design choice to test, not proof of comprehension benefit.

Use the [authoring loop](agent-authoring.md), the [language reference](spec/native-draft.md),
and the [delivery walkthrough](native-review-walkthrough.md). The
[project goal](project-goal.md) defines the broader objective. Current model
comparisons and automated browser checks do not replace human-review observations.
